IEP Meeting Prep Coach Privacy Policy

Generated: June 29, 2026

IEP Meeting Prep Coach is a local-first iOS utility for preparing for an IEP or 504 school meeting.

Data Stored

The PoC stores meeting type, date, grade band, parent-entered concerns, optional pasted plan text, generated question cards, meeting notes, action items, and follow-up draft text on the device.

Local-Only Assumption

This PoC has no account system, cloud sync, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or in-app purchases. Data stays in the app container unless the user exports or shares it.

Export and Deletion

Users can export local JSON data from the Data screen and can delete app data by deleting the app. Demo data can be reset inside the app.
