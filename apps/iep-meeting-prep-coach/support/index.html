IEP Meeting Prep Coach Support

Generated: June 29, 2026

IEP Meeting Prep Coach helps parents prepare for one IEP or 504 meeting by organizing concerns, generating question cards, capturing meeting outcomes, and drafting follow-up email text.

Common Tasks

  • Create or edit a meeting plan from the Setup tab.
  • Accept the educational-use disclaimer before generating a question stack.
  • Pin, reorder, annotate, and check off questions from the Questions tab.
  • Use Meeting Mode to move through prompt cards during a meeting.
  • Capture commitments and generate a follow-up email from the Follow-Up tab.
  • Export or import local JSON data from the Data tab.

Data

The PoC is local-first. It does not use accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or in-app purchases.
