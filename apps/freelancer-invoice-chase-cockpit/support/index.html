Support for Freelancer Invoice Chase Cockpit

Generated: July 1, 2026

Freelancer Invoice Chase Cockpit helps freelancers track unpaid invoices that were created elsewhere, prioritize follow-up, draft editable chase messages, log client promises, and export local invoice follow-up data.

Common Tasks

  • Open the Chase tab to see invoices grouped by due-today, overdue, promised, and due-soon status.
  • Tap Draft Follow-Up to choose a tone and edit a message before copying or sharing.
  • Use Log Promise when a client gives a payment date.
  • Use Mark Paid after payment is received.
  • Use the Data tab to export JSON or CSV.

Current PoC Limits

The app does not create invoices, process payments, send automatic emails, sync with accounting tools, connect bank accounts, provide financial/legal advice, or host a client portal.
