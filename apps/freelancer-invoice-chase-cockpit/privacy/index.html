Privacy Policy for Freelancer Invoice Chase Cockpit

Generated: July 1, 2026

Freelancer Invoice Chase Cockpit is a local-first proof of concept for tracking unpaid invoice follow-up. The current PoC does not create accounts, sync to a server, process payments, connect to banks, send email automatically, run analytics, show ads, or include in-app purchases.

Data Stored

The app stores user-entered client names, contact details, invoice labels, invoice amounts, due dates, source tool notes, payment links, follow-up notes, promised payment dates, and paid/paused status locally on the device as JSON.

Data Sharing

The app can generate editable follow-up messages and hand them to the native share sheet. JSON and CSV exports are created only when the user taps export.

Deletion and Export

Users can export JSON or CSV from the Data tab. A production release should add an explicit delete-all-user-data control before App Store submission.

Contact

Support and privacy requests can be routed through support.
